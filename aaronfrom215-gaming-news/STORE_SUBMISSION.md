- App name: AGamey
- Subtitle: Gaming News Built for Players
- Category: News (secondary: Entertainment)
- Content rating: complete in each store based on linked news content

## Before uploading
- Replace placeholder social links and add a monitored support email.
- Confirm RSS/linking permission for every publisher retained in the production feed.
- Create phone and tablet screenshots from final builds.
- Build signed Android App Bundle and iOS archive using the developer accounts.
- Complete Google Data Safety and Apple App Privacy questionnaires accurately.

## Google Play testing
- Use internal testing first.
- New personal accounts generally require 12 opted-in closed testers for 14 continuous days before production access.
